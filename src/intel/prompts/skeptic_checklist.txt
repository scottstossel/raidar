SKEPTIC CHECKLIST:

1. **Source match** — Does the claim match what the source actually says?
   - Look for misquotes, taken-out-of-context statements, or overstatements.

2. **Benchmark comparison** — Are benchmark comparisons fair?
   - Same dataset? Same evaluation protocol? Same compute budget?

3. **Publication status** — Is this preprint or peer-reviewed?
   - Preprints are valid but less vetted; flag if it's a significant claim on a preprint.

4. **Adoption signals** — Does the claim of "adoption" or "impact" match reality?
   - Are there actual users/citations, or just novelty?

5. **Reproducibility** — Can readers reproduce the work?
   - Code available? Hyperparameters disclosed? Sufficient detail?
